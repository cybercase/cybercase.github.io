# The concept of minimum

It's been a while since my last post on this blog. Too much work and too little spare time :-/

Lately, though, I'm reading a lot! It's much easier for me to find the time to read an article, or the source code of some project, than to write my own code. Writing good code takes quite a lot of time, so I thought I could share some of my reading with the few readers of this blog. Better than nothing, isn't it?

So, let's start with an old Dr. Dobb's article I just finished reading: [Generic\<Programming\>: Min and Max Redivivus](https://web.archive.org/web/20120211204354/http://drdobbs.com/184403774) (archived copy).
I would never have thought that such a simple question, implementing the C min and max macros with C++ templates, would need such a long answer (and so much code).

Even though lately I've started to think less highly of C++ (but that's a topic for another post), I find this kind of problem a great brain teaser.

And look at the author of the article: yes, it's Andrei Alexandrescu, the author of [Modern C++ Design](../inspiration_code/) :)
