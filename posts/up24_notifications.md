### Intro

After my last post on [UP24 reverse engineering](../up24_reverse_engineering/), I received a lot of emails about the Android app I was writing for myself to get notifications on the Jawbone UP24 band.

Over the last month I found the time to write the app and release a [demonstration video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JFcVZpmnbII) of how it works.

### Requirements

- Official UP Application
- A UP24 band paired with your device
- Firmware version 3.0.21 running on your band

### Disclaimer

This app has been widely tested and works correctly: I use it every day to get call and SMS notifications on my wrist.

However, it's important to note that this app is the result of reverse engineering, so:

- It may interfere with the normal functioning of the band
- It may drain your band's battery
- It may stop working anytime (especially after a firmware update)
- It may not work with all band versions
